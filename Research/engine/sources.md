# Research Sources

The canonical cumulative registry is [`../sources/registry.json`](../sources/registry.json). Its maintenance and evidence-tier rules are documented in [`../sources/README.md`](../sources/README.md).

Research runs begin from that registry, recheck due sources and propose additions through a candidate-local `source-registry.patch.json`. Do not rebuild the source list from scratch and do not edit the stable registry during research.
